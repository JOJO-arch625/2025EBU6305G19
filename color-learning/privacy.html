Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Data We Collect

This website does not collect personal data through user accounts, cookies, or tracking technologies. All quiz scores, game progress, and community posts exist only within your current browser session and are automatically cleared when you close the page or navigate away.

How We Use Data

No data is sent to any server or third-party service. All interactions — including test answers, game scores, and discussion posts — happen entirely within your browser using local JavaScript. Nothing is stored, transmitted, or shared externally.

Advertising & Tracking

We do not use:

  • Advertising trackers or ad networks
  • Analytics cookies or third-party analytics scripts
  • Browser fingerprinting technologies
  • Social media tracking pixels

Your Choices & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over your experience:

  • Sound Effects — Toggle on/off using the Sound button in the Game page
  • Hint System — Enable or disable hints in the Game page settings
  • Animations — Disable decorative animations using the Motion toggle in the Game page
  • High Contrast Mode — Switch to high-contrast display using the ◐ button in the navigation bar
  • Font Size — Adjust text size using the A-/A+ buttons in the navigation bar
  • Clear All Data — Simply refresh the page to reset all session data

Community Content

Discussion posts and profile information displayed on the Community pages are simulated front-end data for demonstration purposes only. No real user data is collected or stored. Any posts you create exist only in your current browser session and are not visible to other users or shared with any third party.

Data Retention

Since no data is collected or stored on any server, there is no data retention period. All session data is ephemeral and disappears when you close your browser.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at colorlearning@example.com.
